### Understood
- **UPSERT** (`ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`) — atomically create or update in one query.
- **Atomic update** — protecting against race conditions at the DB level, not in Go.
- **Dependency Inversion** — `RepoMethod` interface lives in `usecase`.
- **Graceful shutdown** — `go func()` is required so main can wait for `ctx.Done()`.
- DTO separation: Request and Response must be different structs.
