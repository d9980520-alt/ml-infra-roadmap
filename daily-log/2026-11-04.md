Understood
- Clean Architecture flow: `main.go` is just the DI container. HTTP/gRPC -> Service -> Repository/Queue.
- `sync.Cond` parks goroutines (no CPU spin) when the queue is empty, which is crucial for high-load systems.
- Graceful shutdown requires `errgroup` to stop the server, close the queue, and wait for workers to finish.
